## Edit style per pillar (Step: "I'm a different person on an EUC")

Each pillar has its own look. What stays the same everywhere: the STEPBAE end card and the stepbae.com line.

| Pillar | Look and pace | Text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WMDA** | Current template: speaker on screen, the person being discussed shown working, wide framing | Gold word-by-word captions, sticker notes, shout-out card | Live commentary |
| **EUC** | Fast and energetic: first-person POV, speed ramps, hard cuts on the beat, 15–30 s | Big punchy kinetic text, speed and distance overlays | Driving music, wind and motor sound |
| **Korea / Japan** | Travel vlog: warm color, location titles with a map pin ("Seoul · Gangnam"), 30–60 s | Clean subtitles, place names | Light music plus natural sound; Step talks to camera |
| **Aspen** | Cinematic: slow shots, wide landscapes, letterbox bars, 45–60 s | Elegant serif titles, few words | Step's voiceover, written by Claude from a theme (e.g. patience, craft, nature's colors as shade matching), calm music |
| **Charlotte lake / paddleboard** | Calm loops: 7–15 s, seamless replay, sunsets | Minimal: one line or none | Water sounds, soft music |

**Voiceover:** Step records the Aspen voiceovers on his phone from scripts Claude writes (about 1 minute of recording per clip, in a quiet room). His real voice, no AI voice clone: authenticity is the point.

## Partner dentist spotlight (proposal, Nov 7: needs Step's decision)

**Goal:** strengthen client relationships and send patients to client practices, so those practices send more cases to us.

**Recommendation: rotate it as a program for all clients, not an ad for one dentist.**
- **Frequency:** about 1 post in 7 carries a spotlight card, so each dentist appears a few times a month, not in every clip. Constant ads read as paid and get skipped.
- **Rotate across all client dentists.** Featuring only one dentist can look like an exclusive tie to that practice to prospective dentists, and it leaves other clients out.
- **Best placement:** personal and local clips (Charlotte, family, EUC around town), where viewers are more likely to be local patients. WMDA clips mostly reach technicians.
- **Stronger than a card:** one dedicated "Meet Dr. ___" clip per client: their office, why they care about quality, a case we made together. The card points to it.
- **stepbae.com/partners:** a page listing every partner practice. The card says "Find a STEPBAE partner dentist," plus that week's featured practice.
- **Sales tool:** "Send us cases and we feature your practice to our audience" becomes a perk when signing new dentists.

**Rules for spotlights:**
- The dentist approves their card and clip before it posts.
- No patient faces, names or case photos without the patient's written consent.
- Follow the dentist's state dental board advertising rules: no "best dentist" claims, and no testimonials unless their board allows them.

## Phases

1. **Nov 9 to Feb 25: build to the talks.** Post the 2nd WMDA clips mixed with personal pillars. Every clip with an AES or GC promo card posts before Feb 25. Run a "come see Step live" push in the last two weeks.
2. **Feb 26 to June: bring in dentists.** Post the 3rd WMDA clips. Promo cards become "Send your first case" or a partner dentist spotlight (card re-render only). Re-post top performers with new hooks.
3. **Summer onward: evergreen.** The rest of the library, themed series, travel and EUC seasons.

## Distribution

- **Featured people:** the day before their clip posts, Step sends them the file ("You're featured tomorrow, feel free to share"). Use an Instagram Collab where possible; tag them everywhere.
- **Vendors:** send GC, Smile Line USA and VITA the clips that show their products.
- **Communities:** the Guild and dental technician Facebook groups. EUC clips can go to EUC rider groups.
- **Crediting the Dental Technicians Guild:** optional (never promised), recommended for goodwill.

## Turning views into cases

- **One link in the bio:** stepbae.com/wmda with three buttons: send a case, see Step live, watch more. (Not built yet.)
- **Tagged links (UTM)** to see which clips bring visits and inquiries.
- **Dentist email campaign:** when it resumes, one dentist-facing clip per email.
- **Insights articles:** each teaching clip becomes an Insights article with the video embedded.

## Monthly check

Track per pillar: 3-second hold rate, watch-through, shares and saves, follows, site clicks, case inquiries, and whether new followers include dentists. Shift the mix and the next edits toward what wins.

## Risks

- **Step's review time.** Step reviews a sample of each batch plus anything Claude flags; the rest follows the template rules.
- **Permissions.** 2016 footage approved by the Guild. Still needed: 2017 footage, the other dental batch.
- **Out-of-date claims.** Fact-check every dentist-facing teaching clip; add a "2026 update" note where needed.
- **Daily posting load.** Mark prepares captions; Step schedules in bulk (Meta Business Suite or similar).
- **Audience drift.** If EUC or travel followers outnumber dentists, tighten the "tie back to teeth" rule or split EUC onto its own TikTok.
- **Dentist spotlights:** board advertising rules and patient consent (see above).
