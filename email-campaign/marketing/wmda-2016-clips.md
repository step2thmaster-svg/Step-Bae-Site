## Left out on purpose

- Moments where the camera is on the crowd or competitors instead of Step.
- Ethnic jokes, drinking and drug jokes, and swearing. They were fine for a live 2016 stream among friends, but not for a lab account dentists check out.

The captions come from automatic transcription, and a few names and product terms were corrected by hand (e.max, Ivoclar, DTG, Dr. Brumm). Watch each clip once for wrong words before posting. Names are the most likely errors.
