## 3. Replying to comments (for Step)

1. Reply in public first, short and warm, the way you'd answer a colleague at the bench. Answer the actual comment.
2. If the commenter is a dentist (check the profile), add one line: "Happy to talk shop. I'll send you a DM."
3. In the DM, ask one question about their work ("What kind of cases do you send out most?"), not a pitch.
4. If they're interested, point them to stepbae.com and info@stepbae.com, and say we'll set up their account by email. No phone calls.
5. Log every dentist who replies in `log.md` so Concierge can take over and Mailman can drop them from the cold sends.
